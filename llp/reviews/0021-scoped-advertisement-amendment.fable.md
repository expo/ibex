#### 2. Overall assessment

This is a strong, unusually honest design package: the join matrix's rows are real (every one of the ~90 file:line citations I checked was accurate at the pin), the Option B recommendation is correct and correctly argued from the actual producer/parser code, the A7 claim wording is rule-8-clean, and the amendment itself surfaced the M18 ownership conflict rather than papering over it. Where it falls short is on its own stated standard — the matrix is "the authoritative gate-code worklist" and M11 "the measured blast radius" — and my independent sweeps found real consumers and pins missing from three enumerations (the M18 path pins, the M11 armed-schema pins, and the report-schema restamp chain that M4 changes touch), plus two precision gaps in M7 that sit directly on the certification-soundness invariant. None of these makes the design unsound as written, and none undermines the Option B direction (the missed M11 pins strengthen it); they make the worklist incomplete and one A10 decision under-informed. All are cheaply fixable in a revision.

**MATERIAL 3 — the report-schema restamp chain has no row.** M4 + the LLP 0032 delta add a `scopeDigest` binding to both report schemas, but `bindings` is `additionalProperties: false` in both `capsec/schema/conformance-report.schema.json` and `schemas/capsec-conformance-report-v2.schema.json`, and the v1 rich-report schema is pinned across the digest contract: `capsec-contract.mjs:503`, `capsec/registry/policy-rules.json:255`, `crates/capsec-semantics/src/canonical.rs:278`, `digest.rs:166`, plus contract example fixtures. **MATERIAL 4 — M7 does not state the scoped form of the summary/fixture-union invariant.** The current soundness core is the exact equation at :1508-1527 (`required == passed == executions`, seven summary equalities). **MATERIAL 5 — the runtime carrier of the lineage anchor is unnamed.** §A5 and M7 say admission "resolves the tuple's currently admitted scope from the checked-in promotion lineage," but M7 is `portable_target_admission.rs` — code that runs inside the armed binary with no repository access. A10 #3 (bundle vs repo placement) is adjacent but does not decide this. *Resolves:* name the runtime-side anchor explicitly in M7/M20 (and note that rollback resistance against a whole-binary rebuild from old sources is out of scope — build provenance's job, M26's layer).

**MATERIAL 6 — the uncertified disposition's type placement is undecided and absent from A10.** Host's cell map today is `BTreeMap<String, TargetCellDisposition>` (`src/host/mod.rs:784`) — the *decision.rs* serialized enum (:395-400). Neither A3 nor A10 names this choice. *Resolves:* add it to A10 (or settle it in A3: host-side type + projection, keeping decision.rs untouched).

**Q2 — Option B substitution-resistance.** Attacks attempted: *(1) stale advertisement + fresh report* — refused today at :1341-1387 (raw-content + digest + 15-binding equality); the scoped re-derivation (M7) adds the scope to the same comparison; F1 pins it. *(2) two tuples' artifacts crossed* — refused by `bindings.target == advertisement.target` (:1362) + tuple-unique selection (:568-580) + `authenticate_local_engine` against the loaded engine; F1's converse arguably covers it, but no fixture explicitly crosses tuples — worth one line in F1. *(3) re-admission after lineage rollback* — closed at promotion time by §A5/M19/F6; at runtime it reduces to Material 5 (the embedded anchor) and, for whole-binary rollback, to build provenance — the amendment should say that boundary out loud. *(4) snapshot from a differently-scoped run* — under Option B the snapshot carries no scope and selects nothing but the tuple (verified: `ExpectedArmingIdentity` :70-109 has no scope field; the tuple+engine join is the only bridge), so there is no degree of freedom; F1's "no snapshot participation" note plus F11 cover it. **Judgment on the trade:** B's "digest never travels alone" argument is sound, and it does *not* relocate trust to admission — admission is already the sole cell-map authority today (`authenticated_target_cells` is the only path into `new_armed`), and B keeps the scope on exactly that channel, re-derived rather than copied. A would add a copy authenticated by the same embedded chain (no independent root), plus the M11 cascade — which my sweep shows is *larger* than priced. B is correctly recommended.

**Q3 — claim wording vs rule 8.** Compliant. Swept the full amendment for "fail-closed"/"refused"/"safe": the only fail-closed assertions are A7's, and both name their layer (startup admission, typed-gate refusal); the physical-entrypoint layer's absence is stated with its reason; the zero-decision remainder is explicitly excluded from both layers; negative controls and adversarial compositions are marked diagnostic/never-upgrading in A7, F4, and F10. "It remains callable" is an availability statement, not a safety claim. A3's uncertified-disposition wording asserts only reached-gate refusal semantics with the mechanism named (`decision.rs:609-621` projection) — no universal negative anywhere.

**Q4 — monotone lineage.** No bypass found within the chain's own threat model. *Retirement abuse:* a "retired" cell still in the live inventory fails (A1/F7); genuine inventory retirement does narrow, but that lane bottoms out in reviewed inventory code changes (the LLP 0044 §2/LLP 0040 accepted posture) — the amendment should state that the guard on that one lane is code review, not the chain. *Rename/split/merge laundering:* mappings must be generated from inventory history, total-and-exactly-once on both sides (F9), and — decisive — admission re-derives the expansion from the intensional definition against the live inventory, so mapping games cannot drop a cell the definition still selects. *Genesis re-declaration:* refused while the lineage records any scope for the tuple (F6's second half); erasing lineage means rewriting checked-in history under the lineage verifier. *Digest domains:* `ibex:capsec:scope:1` / `scope-expansion-diff:1` / `scope-cell-mapping:1` are genuinely separate, companions bound into the scope by digest — separation holds. One residual lane, for the author: a *tuple redefinition* (any feature-list change) creates a fresh tuple with a legitimate genesis scope — lineage is per-tuple, so a narrower scope can ship under a new tuple without tripping anything. The engine really is different (features are part of the authenticated identity, `mod.rs:6733-6737`), so the claim stays honest, but it is the one narrowing-shaped move the chain permits; see open question 3 below.

**Q5 — the A10 open questions.** #1 (telemetry placement): genuine author decision — both designs are sound, the trade (wire-schema restamp vs host-only distinguishability) is fairly stated, and check 3 confirms the recommended option is mechanically real. #3 (bundle vs repo placement): genuine, though it must be decided jointly with Material 5, which it currently doesn't subsume. #4 (`Copy` on `TargetArmState`): genuine implementation choice, correctly constrained ("state and identity inseparable at construction"). #2 (v1-path ownership): the decision is genuinely the author's, but the amendment should have *settled the pin inventory* before posing it — as posed, it is under-informed (Material 1). **Missing from both A6 and A10:** the uncertified-disposition type placement (Material 6) and the runtime lineage-anchor carrier (Material 5). Also arguably missing: a named schema/shape for the M16 introspection surface (it is a new certified-facing surface with no schema id reserved — minor).

#### 6. Open questions for the author beyond A10

1. Should the scoped admission invariant include an explicit **zero-authoritative-contribution rule** for out-of-scope cells (Material 4), and should F4 assert it directly (out-of-scope executions present in the diagnostic schema ⇒ byte-identical authoritative evidence set)?
2. Which artifact carries the **lineage-resolved current scope into the armed binary** — the checked promotion admission marker, the bundle-carried scope artifact via M20, or both with an equality check (Material 5)?
3. Is a **feature-list-induced fresh tuple** (new tuple ⇒ genesis scope, no predecessor) acceptable as the one chain-permitted narrowing-shaped move, or should genesis admission also require that no *prefix tuple* (same triple, different features) has a live scope? Relates to deferred register item 7.
4. When a scope eventually expands to the full inventory, does the tuple stay `ScopedAdvertised` with a total scope, or re-enter `CompleteAdvertised`? M12 says the two are mutually non-constructible per admission result; stating the end-state intent would prevent a future "graduation" shortcut.
5. Do the two workflow-security tests' empty-advertisement assertions become lineage-verifier-style *source-revision* checks under the A10 #2 resolution, or are they retired? (They currently encode "no target is ever advertised" as a permanent invariant of HEAD.)

#### 7. Verdict

**NOT READY** — by a narrow margin, and entirely on completeness-of-enumeration and two precision gaps, not on design direction. The A6/Option B recommendation, the `ScopedAdvertised` design, the A7 wording, and the A5 lineage design are sound and I found no attack the design fails to close within its stated threat model. But this matrix is, by its own §A9 declaration, the authoritative gate-code worklist, and the A10 #2 decision packet is missing four of its pins.
